# Proof page

The public page is the CLI, in the browser. It runs `scoreRecovery`, `LintEngine`, and `preflight` from this repository.

## Scene

Someone at a desk at night, reading a terminal. The page stays dark. One ground, `#101412`. Text is bone `#d7e0d6`. Errors are chalk red, warnings chalk yellow, a pass is chalk green. No second theme, no cream panel, no photograph.

## Type

Schibsted Grotesk for the sentence and the section headings. IBM Plex Mono for commands, diagnostics, and controls. Both are self-hosted. Corners are square. The only motion is the stamp, and it stops when the system asks for reduced motion.

## First screen

The sentence, then `npx mcp-lint recover errors.json` with the sample already scored. Check and preflight follow as the same kind of session. The command list under that is the reference, not a second design.
